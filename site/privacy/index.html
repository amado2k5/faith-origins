Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026.

No accounts, no cookies, no ads, no analytics or tracking. We do not sell or share your personal information.

Who is responsible

Ahmed Abdel Aal, who runs the site on behalf of the author's heirs, is the controller and the person responsible for the protection of personal information, including under Quebec's Law 25. Contact: find@origins.faith.

What happens to your data

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the site and requests pass through Cloudflare, Inc. Both see your IP address and request details in order to deliver and protect the pages (legitimate interests, GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). We do not receive or keep these logs. Both providers are in the United States and rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses for transfers from the EU and UK.
  • On your device only: your browser keeps your choices (language, theme, text size, reading mode and the last page you read) in local storage. They are never sent to us, and they are strictly necessary for features you use, so no consent banner is needed. Fonts are served from this site, so no font service is contacted.
  • Email: if you write to us, we receive your address and message and use them only to answer you, and delete them on request.
  • GitHub: the “Report a typo” and “Tell us about a book” links open a page on GitHub; anything you post there is public and governed by GitHub's terms.

Because we do not track you, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are treated as already honoured. We do not sell or “share” personal information as those words are used in California law.
